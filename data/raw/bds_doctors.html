Doctors, clinics and organisations that treat Dupuytren, Ledderhose or Peyronie’s disease

The following lists contain names and links to Doctors, Surgeons, Hospitals and Clinics providing different treatment options for Dupuytren’s and Ledderhose disease in the UK and ROI. These lists are not exclusive, just those that we are aware of.

For other parts of the world we have some names of doctors on this map. The map is made of doctors who attend conferences or publish articles and/ or who are recommended by patients or other doctors.

DUPUYTREN’S DISEASE

Surgery (fasciectomy)

Fasciectomy is available in most hospitals with an orthopedic or plastic surgery department. It is always good to make sure the surgeon is a member of the BSSH, which can be checked online. The list of surgeons and hospitals we have is by no means exclusive or an endorsement of any hospital or surgeon. A lot of places listed will offer more treatments than the one we have them listed under.

Radiotherapy (for Dupuytren and Ledderhose):

Scotland

In the Beatson Centre in Glasgow patients from West Scotland can receive radiation treatment for Ledderhose (and Dupuytren’s). Referral by an orthopedic surgeon is required, and clinicians are to contact Dr Schipani for more information. This is NHS treatment for patients living in their catchment area only.

Glasgow: Dr I Nixon treats Dupuytren patients, On the NHS only, in the Beatson Centre and in Ross Hall Hospital.

England several locations

Dupuytren’s UK is the private radiotherapy practice of Dr Richard Shaffer, Consultant Clinical Oncologist, providing low-dose radiotherapy for early-stage Dupuytren’s contracture and for Ledderhose disease. Treatment is private only.

 

Initial consultations are held in London, Guildford and Nottingham or Alderley Edge. After consultation, treatment is available at clinics across England, including Alderley Edge (near Manchester), Birmingham, Bristol, Cambridge, Chelmsford, Elstree, Guildford, London, Maidstone, Milton Keynes, Nottingham, Oxford, Portsmouth, Southampton, Wimbledon and Windsor.

 

Dr Shaffer can be contacted via his secretary by email: pa.drshaffer@genesiscare.co.uk  or by phone: 07584 532397.

Dr James Wylie offers private radiotherapy at the Theralife Clinic in Alderley Edge, near Manchester.

Dr De Francesco  treats Dupuytren and Ledderhose patients private in Harley Street/LOC and London Bridge clinics (both HCA clinics). To arrange a consultation email her secretary hazel.evans@hcaconsultant.co.uk
